基礎課程 Foundation

守門人培訓
Gatekeeper Training

🕐 半日 Half-day (3hrs) 👥 10–30 人

學習識別有自殺危機的跡象、如何開口關心，以及轉介求助資源的方法。 適合任何希望幫助他人的人士。

Learn to identify warning signs, start a caring conversation, and connect people to help. Suitable for anyone who wants to support others.

進階課程 Advanced

危機介入培訓
Crisis Intervention Training

🕐 全日 Full-day (7hrs) 👥 8–20 人

深入學習危機評估、溝通策略及即時支援技巧，適合社工、輔導員、教師等專業人士。

In-depth training on crisis assessment, communication strategies and immediate support. Ideal for social workers, counsellors, teachers and other professionals.

社區課程 Community

家庭支援工作坊
Family Support Workshop

🕐 2小時 2hrs 👥 最多 40 人

專為家長、家庭成員設計，學習如何支援有情緒困擾的家人，建立開放溝通的家庭環境。

Designed for parents and family members to learn how to support loved ones in distress and create an open, communicative home environment.
