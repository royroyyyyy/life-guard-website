我們的使命
Our Mission

生命守護致力於透過教育，提升社會各界對自殺危機的識別能力及應對技巧， 讓更多人有能力在關鍵時刻伸出援手，守護每一條寶貴的生命。

Life Guard is dedicated to educating the public on recognising and responding to suicidal crises. We equip individuals with the skills to reach out at the right moment and protect every precious life.

🌱
「每一次關心，都可以是一條生命的轉捩點。」
"Every act of care can be a turning point for a life."
